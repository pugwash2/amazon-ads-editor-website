Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 8, 2025

Ads Command Center LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Ads Command Center desktop application and the website adscommandcenter.io. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Email address: When you join our waitlist or contact us
  • Account information: When you create an account to use our software
  • Support communications: When you contact us for help

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: How you interact with our application (features used, errors encountered)
  • Device information: Operating system, app version
  • Website analytics: Pages visited, referral source (via privacy-respecting analytics)

Amazon Advertising Data

When you connect Ads Command Center to your Amazon Advertising account:

  • We access your campaign data (campaigns, ad groups, keywords, bids, targeting) through the Amazon Ads API
  • Campaign data is stored locally on your device for offline editing
  • We do not store your Amazon campaign data on our servers
  • We do not share your Amazon data with third parties
  • You can revoke access at any time through your Amazon Advertising account settings

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our software and services
  • To communicate with you about updates, features, and support
  • To send waitlist updates and launch announcements (you can unsubscribe anytime)
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve stability
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage and Security

Your Amazon campaign data is stored locally on your computer. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect any data transmitted to our servers (such as account information and usage analytics).

  • All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
  • We do not sell your personal information
  • Access to user data is restricted to authorized personnel only

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Amazon Ads API: To connect to your Amazon Advertising account (governed by Amazon's privacy policy)
  • Email service provider: To send waitlist and account emails
  • Analytics: To understand website usage (no personal data shared)

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Unsubscribe from marketing communications
  • Revoke Amazon API access at any time

6. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. Waitlist emails are retained until you unsubscribe. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or through our application.
