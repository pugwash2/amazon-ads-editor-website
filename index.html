How It Works

1

Connect

2

Download

Pull your campaigns, ad groups, keywords, and targets to your local machine. Works offline from here.

3

Edit

Bulk edit bids, keywords, status, and targeting. Use built-in tools like clone wizard and naming assistant.

4

Review

The Bulksheet Problem

Managing Amazon Sponsored Products campaigns through bulksheets is broken. Download a spreadsheet, make changes offline, upload it back, wait for errors, fix them, upload again. The workflow takes hours and one wrong cell breaks everything.

Amazon's bulk operations tool helps with basic edits, but you're still clicking through their web interface. Change 1,000 keyword bids? That's 1,000 individual operations. The interface times out. You lose progress. You start over.

Third-party Amazon PPC tools cost $200-$10,000/month and focus on automation and AI bidding. But sometimes you just need to edit your campaigns at scale without paying for features you don't use.
